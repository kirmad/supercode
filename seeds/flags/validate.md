---
description: "Pre-operation validation and comprehensive risk assessment"
placement: "before"
---

- Perform pre-operation validation checks
- Calculate risk score: complexity*0.3 + vulnerabilities*0.25 + resources*0.2 + failure_prob*0.15 + time*0.1
- If risk score >0.7, provide detailed warnings
- Check resource availability and constraints
- Validate prerequisites and dependencies
- Suggest safer alternatives if risks are high
